# Motion Video Production

## Workflow
1. Clarify platform, audience, objective, duration, aspect ratio, language, visual references, and available assets.
2. Write a hook-first concept and timed storyboard. For every beat specify shot, duration, framing, motion, on-screen text, voiceover, sound design, and transition.
3. Design for the delivery format: 9:16 vertical, 16:9 landscape, or 1:1 when requested. Keep text inside platform-safe areas.
4. Distinguish user-provided, licensed, original, and placeholder assets. Never present placeholders as real footage.
5. Specify an editable timeline with clip points, keyframes, easing, transitions, captions, audio levels, and export settings.
6. Review pacing, phone readability, subtitle accuracy, audio clipping, visual consistency, and the opening seconds.

## Principles
- Use purposeful movement, smooth easing, consistent timing, and restrained transitions.
- Keep captions concise, synchronized, high-contrast, and accessible.
- Use only music and footage the user supplies or has rights to use.
- Do not promise that a video will go viral.

## Deliverables
Provide a creative brief, storyboard, timeline/edit decision list, asset checklist, caption text when requested, and export checklist. State clearly when a separate video editor or media-generation tool is required.
