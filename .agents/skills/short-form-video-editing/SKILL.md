# Short-form Video Editing
1. Inspect footage and identify strong moments, context, consent, and rights.
2. Select one story or takeaway per clip; do not remove context in a misleading way.
3. Build a 9:16 edit with a strong opening, tight pacing, clear framing, captions, and purposeful ending.
4. Use jump cuts, punch-ins, B-roll, motion graphics, and sound design only when they improve clarity or energy.
5. Generate and verify transcript, names, technical terms, timestamps, and subtitle synchronization.
6. Check safe margins, framing, audio peaks, color consistency, and compression.
7. Export a clean master and platform-specific versions when requested.

For each segment specify source timecode, output duration, crop, text overlay, transition, audio treatment, and reason for inclusion. Never fabricate quotes or imply someone said something they did not. Do not clone a real person's voice or likeness without authorization.
