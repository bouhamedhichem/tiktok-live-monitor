# Visual Brand Design
1. Define purpose, audience, positioning, personality, usage contexts, and accessibility needs.
2. Propose a visual direction with rationale, moodboard keywords, and distinctive concepts.
3. Specify color tokens and accessible pairings, typography hierarchy, spacing, grids, icon style, and image treatment.
4. Design reusable templates for social posts, thumbnails, stories, presentations, or web.
5. Document responsive variants, minimum sizes, safe areas, contrast, and do/don't examples.
6. Check consistency across light/dark backgrounds and mobile/desktop.

Prioritize clarity, hierarchy, contrast, alignment, and consistency. Avoid arbitrary decoration. Do not imitate a living artist's exact style or claim trademark clearance without evidence.

Deliver rationale, HEX palette, typography roles, layout rules, component specs, asset list, and export formats.
