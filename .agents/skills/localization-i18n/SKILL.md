# Localization and Internationalization
1. Identify target locales, dialects, audience, tone, and cultural context.
2. Separate translatable strings from code and avoid concatenating sentence fragments.
3. Support locale-aware dates, numbers, currencies, units, pluralization, and sorting.
4. Test text expansion, fonts, line breaks, and right-to-left layouts.
5. Preserve meaning and product terminology; flag ambiguous source text for review.
6. Avoid stereotypes and culturally inappropriate imagery or examples.
7. Verify navigation, accessibility, and mixed-direction text.
Deliver locale plan, translation-ready strings, RTL checklist, and review notes.
