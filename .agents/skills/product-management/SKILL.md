# Product Management
- Define target users, problem, evidence, value proposition, and non-goals.
- Separate user needs from proposed solutions.
- Write user stories and testable acceptance criteria.
- Prioritize by impact, confidence, effort, risk, and dependencies.
- Identify assumptions, experiments, launch risks, and success metrics.
- Keep scope realistic and document decisions and trade-offs.
Deliver product brief, prioritized backlog, roadmap outline, and measurable launch criteria.
