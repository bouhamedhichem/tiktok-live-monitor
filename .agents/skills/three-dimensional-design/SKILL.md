# 3D Design
1. Define target platform, scale, style direction, polygon or performance budget, and output format.
2. Plan silhouette, proportions, topology, UVs, materials, lighting, and camera.
3. Keep geometry clean and organize objects, collections, naming, and reusable assets.
4. Choose physically plausible materials and lighting when realism is requested.
5. For animation, define rig, key poses, timing, easing, and motion continuity.
6. Optimize meshes, textures, draw calls, and export settings for the target engine.
7. Check artifacts, normals, clipping, texture paths, and licensing.
Deliver scene plan, asset specifications, render settings, and export checklist.
