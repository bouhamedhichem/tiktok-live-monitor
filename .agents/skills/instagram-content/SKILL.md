# Instagram Content
1. Determine format (Reel, Story, carousel, post, Note), audience, voice, language, goal, and brand style.
2. For Reels, provide hook, short script, 9:16 shot plan, subtitles, cover title, caption, CTA, and relevant hashtags.
3. For Stories, design a sequence with one clear message per frame; use interactive stickers only when useful.
4. For carousels, structure a strong first slide, one idea per slide, useful progression, and a final action prompt.
5. For profiles, align avatar, bio, highlights, covers, colors, typography, and grid rhythm.
6. Keep copy natural and concise; offer tone variants when useful.

Rules: Avoid hashtag stuffing and engagement bait; keep text legible and inside safe areas; use licensed media; never promise reach or followers; obtain consent before posting identifiable people.

Deliver: format, creative direction, exact copy, visual layout, CTA, and publishing checklist.
