# Repurpose content across formats and channels

## When to use
Use for converting long content into short posts, clips, and summaries.

## Workflow
1. Identify source claims.
2. Extract themes.
3. Generate channel-specific drafts.
4. Preserve meaning.
5. Adapt length and format.
6. Check rights and attribution.
7. Route for review.
8. Track source-to-output lineage..

## Safety and quality requirements
- Do not invent quotes, facts, or permissions.
- Use least privilege and keep credentials in the platform's secret manager.
- Make side effects observable, auditable, and reversible where possible.
- Validate assumptions against the actual platform version and enabled integrations.
- Include a test plan, failure handling, and a concise operator handover.

## Expected deliverables
- Workflow or architecture diagram and a clear trigger-to-outcome description.
- Configuration or implementation steps with explicit dependencies.
- Input/output contracts, permissions, and environment requirements.
- Positive, negative, duplicate, and recovery test cases with results.
- Monitoring, maintenance, rollback, and ownership notes.
