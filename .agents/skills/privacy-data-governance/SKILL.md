# Privacy and Data Governance
- Inventory personal data, purpose, source, recipients, storage, and retention.
- Collect only what is necessary and document lawful basis/consent requirements with qualified review where needed.
- Define access controls, encryption, audit trails, retention limits, and deletion workflows.
- Minimize data in logs, analytics, prompts, and third-party integrations.
- Provide clear user notices and data export/correction/deletion processes where applicable.
- Assess cross-border transfers, vendor processors, and jurisdiction-specific obligations.
- Test privacy controls and incident response.
Do not provide legal certification. Flag jurisdictional questions for qualified counsel and distinguish technical controls from legal compliance.
