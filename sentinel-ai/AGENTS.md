# SentinelAI engineering guidance

Before changing this application, consult the compatible repository skills:
- `.agents/skills/react-frontend-engineering/SKILL.md`, `ui-ux-product-design/SKILL.md`, and `web-accessibility/SKILL.md` — console UX and accessibility.
- `.agents/skills/cybersecurity-review/SKILL.md`, `owasp-application-security/SKILL.md`, and `application-security-testing/SKILL.md` — secure implementation and verification.
- `.agents/skills/database-schema-design/SKILL.md` and `database-security/SKILL.md` — tenant isolation and data protection.
- `.agents/skills/security-alert-triage-automation/SKILL.md` and `security-incident-response/SKILL.md` — incident workflow design.
- `.agents/skills/machine-learning-engineering/SKILL.md` — model lifecycle, evaluation and monitoring.
- `.agents/skills/software-testing-qa/SKILL.md` and `website-security-engineering/SKILL.md` — testing and production hardening.

Keep the simulator clearly marked as synthetic, do not claim industrial prediction accuracy without validated real-world data, and only ingest telemetry from systems the operator is authorized to monitor.
