# NexusERP engineering guidance

Before changing this application, consult the compatible repository skills:
- `.agents/skills/react-frontend-engineering/SKILL.md` — component architecture and frontend implementation.
- `.agents/skills/ui-ux-product-design/SKILL.md` and `.agents/skills/design-systems-engineering/SKILL.md` — product UX and design consistency.
- `.agents/skills/accessibility-audit/SKILL.md` — accessible interactions and forms.
- `.agents/skills/crm-automation/SKILL.md` — customer lifecycle workflows.
- `.agents/skills/database-engineering/SKILL.md`, `database-schema-design/SKILL.md`, and `database-security/SKILL.md` — persistence, tenant isolation and access controls.
- `.agents/skills/analytics-data-visualization/SKILL.md` and `sql-analytics-engineering/SKILL.md` — reporting correctness.
- `.agents/skills/software-testing-qa/SKILL.md` — test planning and regression checks.

Keep business records tenant-scoped, validate server-side, audit sensitive mutations, and never imply that sample data is connected to a live ERP database.
